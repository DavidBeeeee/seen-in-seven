AI Technology & Responsible Use

The Momentum Hub applications (including StorySculpt, Next Step Navigator, and AI Boardroom) use modern artificial intelligence models (such as DeepSeek and language intelligence engines) to assist you with scriptwriting, business diagnostics, and strategic reflection.

  • Assistance, Not Automation: AI is provided as a thinking partner and accelerator. You remain the creator, editor, and decision-maker for all content, scripts, and business directions produced.
  • Confidentiality: Do not enter passwords, credit card numbers, confidential proprietary trade secrets, or protected personal health information into prompt boxes or chat inputs.
  • Output Verification: While our engines are built with strict factual and style boundaries, AI generated drafts should always be reviewed by you before publication or external use.

Data Handling & Privacy

Your privacy and the security of your work are taken very seriously.

  • Account Data: Your profile information, saved scripts, business answers, and Next Move action items are stored securely in our encrypted cloud database (Supabase).
  • Ownership: You retain full intellectual property ownership of the scripts, business strategies, and copy you generate and edit inside The Momentum Hub.
  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, email address, or project contents to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

Pricing, Access, & Cancellation

Access to The Momentum Hub apps is provided through active Colorado Mastermind memberships and program entitlements.

  • Clear Terms: Membership pricing, billing cadences, and renewal intervals are clearly presented upon enrollment.
  • Cancellation Anytime: You may cancel your subscription at any time without hassle or penalty. Upon cancellation, your access remains active through the end of your current paid billing period.
  • Data Retention: If your membership lapses, your saved scripts and workspace history remain securely stored in case you decide to return, until you request deletion.
