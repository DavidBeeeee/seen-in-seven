Terms of Service & Frequently Asked Questions

How access, saved work, AI tools, and support work in The Momentum Hub.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some recommended tools have affiliate links. If you buy through one, David Bee may receive a referral commission. Check the provider's checkout for the actual price, trial, and purchase terms; a recommendation does not promise a discount.

AI Technology & Responsible Use

StorySculpt, Next Step Navigator, and AI Boardroom use third-party AI services to help with writing, business questions, and reflection. When you ask for an AI response, the app sends relevant input and conversation context to its AI provider.

  • Assistance, Not Automation: AI is provided as a thinking partner and accelerator. You remain the creator, editor, and decision-maker for all content, scripts, and business directions produced.
  • Confidentiality: Do not enter passwords, credit card numbers, confidential proprietary trade secrets, or protected personal health information into prompt boxes or chat inputs.
  • Review Before Use: AI can make mistakes or invent details. Check drafts, claims, and recommendations before publishing them or acting on them.

Data Handling & Privacy

Saved work is cloud-hosted, not a private file that only your device can read.

  • Storage: Account information and saved app work are stored in Supabase's managed PostgreSQL database. Sign-in and database access rules control member access. This is not end-to-end encrypted storage that hides your work from the service operator.
  • Who Can See Your Work: David Bee has administrator access to member records and saved work for support and running the Studio. The hosting, database, and AI providers also process data needed to provide their services. Do not enter information you need to keep secret from those services.
  • Your Copies: Keep your own copy of any work you need outside the Studio. AI output is not a guarantee of accuracy, originality, or exclusive rights.
  • Deletion Requests: Email email@davidbee.me from your account address and say whether you want a particular project or your account data deleted. David handles the request; this page does not perform an automatic deletion. Requesting deletion does not itself cancel billing.

Pricing, Access, & Cancellation

Access to The Momentum Hub apps is provided through active Colorado Mastermind memberships and program entitlements.

  • Billing Terms: Check the offer and checkout you purchased for price, renewal, and refund terms. This page does not change those terms.
  • Cancellation and Access: Contact David at the email below for help canceling. When the Studio processes a cancellation event from Systeme, it removes access supplied by that purchase. There is no automatic wait until the end of the paid billing period. Access from a separate active purchase or a manual grant can remain.
  • Saved Work After Cancellation: Removing access does not automatically delete saved scripts or workspace records. Ask David if you want them deleted. Retained work is not a promise of unlimited storage or permanent access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the different Momentum Hub tools?

Sign into your Colorado Mastermind Studio account and use the sidebar. Each tool checks your access separately; being signed in does not mean every tool is included in your purchase.

Can I switch between dark and light color modes?

Yes. Click the sun or moon icon in the top header on any page to switch between our custom dark and light themes.

What should I do if a tool fails to generate or save?

Copy any unsaved text before refreshing, then check your connection and try again. If it still fails, email David with the tool name and what happened. There is no guaranteed immediate response or repair time.

How do Certainty Sessions work?

Certainty Sessions are working sessions with David Bee. Members with access can request an available slot on the Certainty Sessions page. Check the booking's status and session details before treating it as confirmed.
