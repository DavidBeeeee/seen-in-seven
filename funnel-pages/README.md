# funnel-pages/ is an archive, not a mirror

These files record custom-code blocks that were pasted into Systeme at some
point. Systeme is the system of record for every one of them. Nothing here is
evidence of what a customer currently reads, and on 2026-09-15 almost none of it
even resembles what a customer currently reads.

**Before you quote a line from this directory, fetch the page.** On 2026-09-14 a
morning run read `eee-sales-page.html`, reported a $127 price and EEE branding as a
live read of `/yeees`, and put it in front of David. The served page is The
Momentum Hub at $250 and had been for some time. WBR-343.

## What the numbers say

- Eleven files could be matched to a served page and had drifted past recognition:
  all seven day pages, the `/777challenge` landing and both opt-in blocks,
  `/kickoff`, `/victory` and `eee-sales-page.html`. These are not edited copies of
  the live pages. They are an earlier generation of the funnel.
- The two `/seeninseven` blocks match about half. That is the closest anything here
  comes to current, and half a page is still not a source.
- Twenty-six files have no identifiable served page, including the whole `app-page*`
  set and the `sis-page3` through `sis-page6` blocks. They may belong to funnels
  that are switched off. They were not compared and are marked UNVERIFIED rather
  than current.

`backups/2026-08-06-live/` is a deliberate pre-launch snapshot and is left alone.

## The standing rule

A claim about a customer-facing page is fetched in the same breath as the claim.
This directory can tell you what was once true. It cannot tell you what is true.
