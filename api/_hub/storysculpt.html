Help & privacy

Who makes this?

StorySculpt is part of The Momentum Hub. David Bee built it and supports it.

What happens to my work?

Your chats, project titles, notes, finished scripts, and story profile are saved to your Studio account so you can return to them. When you ask StorySculpt to continue or revise, your title, profile, notes, and recent messages are sent to DeepSeek to generate the next question or draft. Do not enter anything you do not want processed by an AI service.

You can delete a chat and its saved script with the Delete button inside that chat. Your standing story profile is separate and can be edited from Story profile.

Where do I get help?

For a question about a script, chat, or your story profile, email email@davidbee.me and say what you were doing. For Studio sign-in, access, or billing, use the Studio home and its account support options, or email the same address with “Studio account” in the subject.
