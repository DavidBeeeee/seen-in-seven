Turn raw thoughts into video scripts that sound like you.

Getting on camera is hard when a blank page stares back at you, or when generic tools turn your thoughts into stiff corporate slogans. StorySculpt solves that. You talk through raw thoughts in a focused interview, and the system shapes your exact phrasing into a ready-to-film script.

1

1. Why the 5E interview keeps your voice

Generic tools guess what you mean and flatten your personality. StorySculpt asks 4 to 6 focused questions to draw out the core emotional direction of your video: Entertainment, Enragement, Epiphany, Empathy, or Education.

It keeps your actual phrasing, natural cadence, and rough opinions so the finished script sounds like you talking to a friend.

2

2. What to expect: 5 to 10 minutes

An interview takes about 5 to 10 minutes across 4 to 6 conversational turns. No preparation or polished outline is required: unedited thoughts and honest rants give the framework the real detail it needs.

Pick a format below, answer each question in your own words, and pause whenever you want. Your work saves as you go.

3

3. What happens when your script is ready

Your finished script appears in the script panel with line-by-line editing, instant note refinement, and one-click export for your teleprompter.

When you record, mark the script filmed to track your momentum. Build a steady library of filmed videos, keep your story profile updated, and start your next script anytime.

A lapsed membership pauses new scripts, interview generation, and revisions while keeping your saved chats, scripts, and story profile intact.

What happens to my work, and what does StorySculpt remember?

Your chats, project titles, notes, finished scripts, and story profile are saved to your Studio account so you can return to them. When you ask StorySculpt to continue or revise, your title, profile, notes, and recent messages are processed by the AI model that writes your next question or draft, guided by David's method and your own words. Do not enter anything you do not want processed by an AI service.

StorySculpt's memory matches only what is stored in your account: (1) your standing story profile in storysculpt_member_context (the fields you save in Settings & story profile plus any facts or voice notes you explicitly confirm from a finished project, which automatically stop resurfacing after 90 days), (2) the current chat in storysculpt_projects (its conversation turns, chat-specific notes, UGC product findings, saved script versions, and stored material separated into verified member facts, tentative inferences, and generated phrasing so guesses and model lines are never reused as facts), and (3) the opening lines of up to 20 of your recent finished scripts, read only so new hooks do not repeat your earlier openings. When a new chat starts, StorySculpt shows which saved profile entries it will use so you can leave any entry out for that chat without deleting it from your standing profile.

Step away whenever you like. Nothing is deleted when you stop: your chats and scripts stay in your account until you delete them.

Can I change the format?

The format is locked after creation. Start a new script to use a different format; your existing chat stays in your list.

Can I pause and come back?

Yes. Leaving does not delete your project. Reopen the chat to resume the interview where you stopped. If a step is interrupted before StorySculpt replies, your last answer is kept and you can resume from that exact step. If an opening answer covers several topics at once, StorySculpt asks one narrowing question so you can pick the single moment to focus on. Check the save indicator before closing the browser: Saving means a write is pending, Saved means it was acknowledged, and Not saved means the latest change has not reached your account yet. Changes kept only on this device are not available on another device until saved.

Why does StorySculpt ask who this is for?

Before or during a script, you can optionally name who the video is for and what you want that viewer to do next. StorySculpt saves that objective on the script itself and shows it at the top of your finished script so you can check the draft against the audience and next step you intended. If you leave those fields blank, StorySculpt says plainly that no objective was stated instead of guessing one for you. You can add or edit the current script's objective anytime in Settings & story profile.

What does finishing a script mean?

Finishing closes the interview to new turns and assembles your answers into a script you can record. Once a script is finished, the interview is locked so your script stays stable. A new answer cannot be added to a finished interview; use Refine with a note to change the script, or start a new chat for your next script. Copy it or download the text file when you are ready to film. Your saved script does not expire; it stays in your account until you delete it.

How long does an interview take?

An interview takes about 5 to 10 minutes across 4 to 6 conversational turns. You talk through raw, unedited thoughts one question at a time in your natural phrasing, without needing an outline, slide deck, or prepared notes. Rough answers give the 5E framework the real emotional tension and personal perspective your video needs.

What happens after my first script?

When your first script is finished, it is ready to record. You can copy it directly to your teleprompter or download the text file to film your video. Once you film, mark it filmed or start your next script to build momentum. Your saved scripts stay in your library and your standing story profile carries forward what StorySculpt learned about your voice and audience into every new script you create.

Can I archive or delete a chat?

Leaving pauses your chat; it does not delete anything. Check that your changes say Saved before closing the browser. Archive puts a chat away without deleting its notes, answers or script. Use the Archive button beside its title, then open Archived and choose Restore chat to bring it back to All. Delete is different: after you confirm, it permanently removes the chat, its versions, and its saved script.

What happens when I delete a project?

Deleting a project permanently removes its row in storysculpt_projects (the chat conversation, notes, material categories, recorded versions, and finished script), cascades deletion to its project context history in storysculpt_context_versions, and clears any unsaved local copy on this device. When you delete a single chat, you get a ten-second undo window where the row shows Deleted and an Undo button to restore it immediately. After that window lapses, the server delete runs and the work is permanently gone. Returning after a delete shows your remaining active chats, or the clean start screen if you have no other chats.

How do I delete all my StorySculpt data, and what is retained?

Deleting all of your StorySculpt data is a self-serve member action. Use the Delete all StorySculpt data button below or in Settings & story profile and confirm once. That action immediately clears and deletes your standing story profile and confirmed memories (storysculpt_member_context), permanently deletes every active, filmed, and archived StorySculpt chat and script (storysculpt_projects), cascades deletion to all project context history (storysculpt_context_versions), and clears StorySculpt's local browser storage on this device.

What is kept after you delete your StorySculpt data: (1) the append-only profile version history (storysculpt_context_versions), including earlier non-empty versions. Delete all adds an empty profile marker ({}) as the latest entry so deleted profile words are never read back into any generation, but it does not erase earlier profile-history rows, (2) count-only operational telemetry in public.logs (which records event types, timestamps, step names, and sentence or category counts, never your words, prompts, or scripts), and (3) your Studio login and membership record (public.users and studio_entitlements) so your other Momentum Hub apps keep working. If you also want your entire Studio login and billing record deleted across every Momentum Hub app, email David Bee at email@davidbee.me with “Studio account deletion” in the subject.

What should I do next?

What if something hard comes up while I write?

Stories often touch painful moments, and ordinary difficulty, honest product caveats, and your own personal experience are always welcome. When an input crosses a safety or high-risk boundary, StorySculpt pauses before spending quota or calling the AI model:

Crisis or self-harm: If something you type says you are thinking about ending your life, hurting yourself, or hurting someone else, StorySculpt does not write the next step. It pauses and shows free, confidential lines you can reach right now: call or text 988 in the US and Canada, 116 123 (Samaritans) in the UK and Ireland, 13 11 14 (Lifeline) in Australia, or findahelpline.com anywhere else. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. Nothing you wrote is deleted, and the chat is there when you are ready to come back. StorySculpt is an AI writing tool, not a counselor.

Medical or financial claims in UGC or offer copy: Before generating, StorySculpt flags unsubstantiated promises that a product cures, treats, heals, or prevents a medical condition, or promises guaranteed income, risk-free profit, or specific earnings. Reframe medical claims around your own personal comfort or everyday routine, and reframe financial claims around the specific task or workflow the product helps with, then generate again.

Disclosures about someone else: If your notes name another person alongside a private medical diagnosis, addiction, arrest, bankruptcy, secret debt, or confidential secret shared without their permission, StorySculpt pauses before generating so you can remove identifying details or focus on your own side of the lesson.
