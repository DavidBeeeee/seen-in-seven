FRAMEWORK
Part 1 | The Hook
The hook is the most important part of your video! It's the first thing you say at 
the beginning of your video. And it's how people determine whether they're 
going to keep watching or not. If you skip the hook and just start telling the 
story or context, people will just scroll to the next video in their feed.
Here's some of our best performing hooks:
• The #1 secret to ___ is…
• Here’s how to ___…
• If you’re struggling with ___, this is your problem... Did you know…
• How to ___ without ___…
• This is probably going to ruffle some feathers…
• Everything you knew about ___ is wrong…
• Here are 3 tips to ___…
• Here’s one simple hack to ___…
• Don’t make this mistake…
• Here’s the secret to___...

Part 2 | The Story or Context
This is where you give your viewer the context or story behind what you're 
about to tell them. This is crucial because it shows them WHY it's important 
to them. Without a story, your content has no meaning, and your audience 
won't take it seriously. Stories are also a really great way to get people to keep 
watching, because once our brains get into a story we have to finish it!
Here are some prompts to help you come up with a story idea:
• So a few weeks ago... [tell the story of how you learned it]
• Too many people do ___ when they should do ___... [give the context of 
what most people are doing wrong, and what they should do instead]
• In the book ___ by ___, it says... [share a quote from a book]
• People always ask me... [answer a question that you get asked a lot]
• So I know this might sound crazy, but... [tackle a false belief you know 
they'll have about your content]

Part 4 | The Offer or CTA
You ALWAYS want to make sure you end your videos with some sort of 
Offer or Call To Action (CTA). Without it, the content just feels lifeless and 
meaningless. If your goal as a movement leader is to get people to change 
their lives, then INVITE them to take ACTION! But it doesn't have to be 
anything crazy. The CTA can be as simple as asking them to follow you for 
more content.
So if you're a [target market name] and you want to get [result or benefit], 
make sure to [fill in one of the examples below]...
Here are some examples:

• Follow for more tips

• Go to ___.com to get___.

BONUS: Video Shooting Tips
You don't need fancy video equipment to get started making videos! 
Remember, consistency is more important than quality at first. Through 
consistency, your videos will get better and better over time automatically. 
Then, when you're at the stage where you're ready to really grow your brand 
and your movement, you can invest in a professional team to create your 
content for you.
Here are some tips for shooting high quality videos on your phone:
• Look for good lighting! If you’re shooting indoors, sit next to a window 
for the best light.
• Tap on the camera view and drag the exposure down just a little bit to 
avoid bright highlights in your shot.
• Turn on "Cinematic Mode" on your phone to get a professional blurry 
background.
• Frame your face so it's about 1/3 from the top of the screen, so it 
doesn't get cut off by your username on the platform.
• Put your phone on a tripod or have a friend hold it, and back up a little 
bit so your face isn't so big in the frame.
• Make sure you have "high quality uploads" turned on in TikTok and 
Instagram
• Use "Auto Captions" when you upload your video to TikTok, Instagram, 
or YouTube to put captions on it.
