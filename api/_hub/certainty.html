When life gets in the way

What happens if you miss a week, drift off, or leave.

You miss a week

Each week runs Monday to Sunday, Eastern time. A week you do not use does not roll over, and nothing else happens. Next week's session is waiting, same as always.

You stop booking for a while

Once three weeks go by without a session, this page opens with a welcome back, shows where you left off, and your weekly session is still included. There is no penalty and nothing to make up.

You need a different time

Rescheduling releases your current time first, so someone else can book it before you pick a new one. Pick the new time right away if you want to keep this week.

Your membership ends

Booking closes with it. Cancel any upcoming session you will not keep before then, so the time goes to someone else. Your past sessions stay on your account, so if you come back they are still here.

This permanently removes your past and cancelled sessions, their topics, and your remembered phone number. An upcoming session stays booked.

The weekly session

Book your weekly session at a time that works for you.
Bring the real thing you are working on or stuck on.
We make progress on it together, live, in the session.

Not a calendar link. A scheduler books you a meeting with whoever is free. This is your standing weekly seat with David: one two-hour working session every week of your membership, and this page remembers what you brought before.

What counts: a session is a success when it is held, meaning you and David sat down and worked on your thing. Those are the sessions your history counts.

One session per week is included with your membership.

Private by default: your booking details are saved to your Studio account and are not visible to other members. Need help? Email email@davidbee.me.

Who reads your topic: only David. It is there so he knows what you are walking in with. Other members never see it.

What sessions are not for: they are working sessions for you and your business. They are not therapy or crisis support, and they are not medical, legal or financial advice. If you are in distress, call or text 988.
